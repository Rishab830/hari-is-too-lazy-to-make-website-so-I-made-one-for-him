Local gardeners opened a weekend seed exchange where residents can trade vegetable, herb, and flower seeds while learning simple ways to start balcony planters.

## Small Packets, Big Plans

The first table filled quickly with labelled envelopes of okra, basil, marigold, spinach, and red amaranth. Volunteers also brought soil trays so children could plant a few seeds before taking them home.

Organizers plan to repeat the exchange on the first Sunday of every month and add a composting demonstration before winter.
