Engineering students at Northside College unveiled a solar-powered service cart designed for campus maintenance crews, with a compact battery system and a frame built from recycled parts.

## Testing On Campus

The cart carried tools, paint cans, and gardening supplies across the main quadrangle during a demonstration on Friday. Faculty advisers said the next test will measure battery life during repeated hill climbs.

The team hopes to share the plans with two nearby schools that want low-cost carts for library and sports equipment.
